Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-03

Kvint does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third-party server.

What Kvint Handles

Display name

When you use Kvint, the app generates a random display name for you (for example, "brave-otter"). You can change this name in Settings. Your display name is:

  • Stored locally on your device, in the app's private Documents folder.
  • Visible to nearby players on your local Wi-Fi network via mDNS while you are in the lobby. It is never sent to a server or retained by anyone.

Networking

Kvint uses iroh for peer-to-peer connectivity. When you use the "Invite a friend" feature to play across different networks, iroh connects through number0's relay infrastructure (relay.iroh.network) to traverse NAT firewalls. In this process:

  • Your IP address and an ephemeral, randomly-generated session ID are observable to number0's relay servers in order to establish the connection. The session ID is regenerated on every app launch and is not linked to your identity.
  • Your display name and game moves are end-to-end encrypted between you and your opponent, and are not visible to the relay.
  • number0's privacy policy applies to their relay infrastructure: https://www.n0.computer/privacy

Game moves (board coordinates) are transmitted directly to your opponent over an encrypted connection. No move history is stored or uploaded anywhere.

What Kvint Does Not Collect

  • No account, email address, or Apple ID
  • No persistent device identifiers
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising data
